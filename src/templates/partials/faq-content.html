No. Whitestone Civil Engineering is a design consultancy. We prepare the calculations, drawings and cost information; your builder or contractor carries out the work.

{% endset %} {% set building_control %}

Our structural calculations and drawings are prepared for submission to building control, whether that is your local authority or a registered building control approver. We answer their questions about our design. The approval decision rests with the building control body.

Planning permission is a separate matter. The Planning Portal’s guidance explains the difference for projects in England and Wales.

{% endset %} {% set estimate_vs_quote %}

No. An estimate is a reasoned forecast of likely cost based on the drawings and information available. A builder’s quote is their offer to do the work for a price. An estimate helps you set a budget and compare quotes, but actual prices depend on the builder, the market and the final specification.

{% endset %} {% set sketches %}

Yes. Hand sketches need dimensions marked on them. PDFs exported from CAD software can often be converted accurately; scanned drawings are redrawn, and we check key measurements and tell you if a scan is not reliable enough to draw from.

{% endset %} {% set consultancies %}

Yes. We provide AutoCAD drafting, structural design support and quantity surveying to engineering consultancies and structural engineers on a subcontract basis, working to your drawing standards where you provide them.

{% endset %} {% set areas %}

We are based in London and work on projects across England, and we consider projects elsewhere in the UK. Much of the work can be done from drawings and photographs. Where a site visit is needed, we agree it in the quote.

{% endset %} {% set formats %}

Drawings are supplied as PDF for review and printing, and as AutoCAD DWG files for editing. Calculations and cost estimates are supplied as PDF.
