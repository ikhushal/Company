We want as many people as possible to be able to use this website. It is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. That means you should be able to:

  • zoom in up to 400% without content being cut off or needing to scroll sideways
  • navigate the whole website using a keyboard
  • use the website with a screen reader
  • use your browser’s settings to change text size and turn off animation
