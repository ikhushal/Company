Design and consultancy only

Whitestone Civil Engineering does not carry out construction work. We prepare the structural calculations, drawings and cost information that your builder, contractor and building control body work from.

Some projects also need input from other specialists, such as a party wall surveyor, a planning consultant or a geotechnical engineer. Where that applies, we will tell you early, so it is in your budget and your timescale from the start.
