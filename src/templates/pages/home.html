Why clients choose Whitestone

  • Direct contact with the engineer

    You deal with the engineer doing the work, from the first call to the final drawings, so nothing is lost in hand-overs.

  • Flexible project sizes

    From a single steel beam for a home renovation to structural design and cost planning for a medium-sized development.

  • Competitive, written quotes

    A written quote before work starts, setting out the scope, the deliverables and the fee, so there are no surprises.

  • Reliable delivery

    Timescales agreed at the start, with updates as the work progresses and straight answers if anything changes.

How a project works

  1. Enquiry

    Tell us about the project and send any drawings, sketches or photographs you have.

  2. Written quote

    We confirm what is needed and send a quote with the scope, deliverables, fee and timescale.

  3. Design

    We prepare the calculations, drawings or estimate. If something is unclear, we ask rather than assume.

  4. Your review

    You review the draft and tell us what needs to change. The quote states the revisions included.

  5. Delivery

    Final documents are issued as PDF and AutoCAD files, ready for your builder and building control.
