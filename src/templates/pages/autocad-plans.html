Converting existing drawings

PDF to AutoCAD
PDFs exported from CAD software can often be converted accurately. Scanned or photographed drawings are redrawn, and key dimensions are checked against the information you provide.
Hand sketch to AutoCAD
Sketches are redrawn to scale from the dimensions marked on them. Where a dimension is missing, we ask rather than guess.
Updating existing CAD files
Changes to your existing drawings, issued as a new, numbered revision.

Revisions

Every issued drawing carries a revision letter and a short note of what changed, so everyone works from the current version.

Your quote states how many rounds of changes are included. Further changes are quoted before the work is done.

Formats and standards

Drawings are supplied as AutoCAD DWG files for editing and as PDF for review and printing.

If you work to particular layer, title block or sheet standards, send them with your enquiry and we will follow them.

Send files with your quote request. For large files, we will suggest a suitable way to transfer them.
